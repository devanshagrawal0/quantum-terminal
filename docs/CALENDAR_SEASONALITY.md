# Crypto Calendar-Effect Movement Sheet (BTC + ETH, 2017→2026)

Built from **9 years of daily history** (Binance, 3,295 days) — `scripts/seasonality.py`.
The question: how does crypto actually move around the calendar — day of week, month,
turn-of-month, options expiry, holidays, earnings season — things we'd been ignoring.

**How to read the significance:** `t` = mean ÷ standard-error. `|t|>2` (**) ≈ significant,
`>2.6` (***) strong, `>1.6` (*) suggestive. **vs-base t** asks the tradeable question: is
this bucket *different from a normal day*? Small samples (specific holidays ≈ 8–9 years of
data) are suggestive at best — always read the `n`. Baseline: BTC +0.089%/day, ETH +0.064%/day.

---

**Now covers 12 coins** (BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, DOGE, ADA, AVAX, LINK, LTC, DOT, MATIC) +
the macro dates. Visual heatmap: `docs/movement_sheet.html` (regenerated by the script).

## The BIGGEST finding — FOMC days (macro, 12-coin, 2020→2026)

**Crypto rallies on FOMC decision days, across the entire board.** Every one of the 12 coins
is positive on the day the Fed announces: **+0.66% to +2.39%**, several individually
significant (MATIC +2.39\*\*\*, ADA +1.71\*\*, LINK +1.43\*\*, LTC +1.36\*\*, SOL +1.67\*, AVAX
+1.60\*, DOT +1.35\*, BTC +1.01\*). Twelve-for-twelve positive is itself strong evidence —
this is a real risk-on / relief pattern into the decision. **The day AFTER (FOMC+1) mostly
gives some back** (negative for most), so the edge is the day-of, not a hold.

**CPI days are NOT directional** — small, insignificant (t ≈ 0–1.2). CPI is a *volatility*
event (can beat or miss), not a lean. Trade the vol, not a side. (CPI dates approximate ±1–2d;
FOMC dates exact.)

**Chinese New Year window is broadly green** (BNB +1.49\*\*, LTC +1.31\*\*, DOT +1.04\*).
**Turn-of-month and earnings-season weeks are mildly green** across most coins.

## The patterns that are REAL (survive significance)

| Pattern | BTC | ETH | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **October ("Uptober")** | **+0.50%/day, t +3.1***, vs-base +2.6** | +0.15% (weak) | **BTC's strongest month, real & strong.** The single best calendar signal. |
| **Thursday weakness** | −0.34%/day, vs-base t −2.1** | **−0.54%/day, vs-base t −2.4** | Real on both. Thursdays underperform a normal day. |
| **Chinese New Year (−5..+2d)** | **+0.89%/day, t +2.1**, vs-base +1.9* | (n=72) | BTC is UP into/around CNY — opposite of the "Asian pre-CNY selling" folklore. |
| **Earnings-season weeks** (12–26 of Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct) | **+0.30%/day, t +2.1** | (risk-on) | Crypto rises during TradFi earnings weeks — risk-on spillover. |
| **Turn-of-month** (last1+first3) | +0.23% (t +1.9*) | **+0.36%, t +2.3** | Real for ETH, mild for BTC. Month-boundary bid. |
| **Post-quarterly-expiry drift** (3d after last-Fri Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec) | +0.24% (weak) | **+0.62%, t +2.2** | ETH drifts up after quarterly expiry; BTC muted. |
| **June weakness** | −0.24% (t −1.1) | **−0.47%, vs-base t −2.1** | Summer soft patch, real for ETH. |
| Wednesday strength (BTC) | +0.35%, t +2.1** | — | Mid-week bid. |
| Saturday strength (ETH) | — | +0.30%, t +2.0** | Weekend retail bid on ETH. |

## The patterns that are NOISE (don't trade them)

- **Santa rally** (Dec 24–Jan 2): positive (BTC +0.40%, ETH +0.82%) but **not significant**
  (n=90, t≈1.0–1.9). A nice story, too few years to trust.
- **Options-expiry day** (monthly or quarterly): nothing on the day itself — the drift is
  *after* quarterly expiry (ETH), not on it.
- **US federal holidays ±1d**: mildly positive, not significant.
- **Thanksgiving week**: flat/noise.
- Most individual months (Jan/Feb/Mar/May/Aug/Sep/Nov/Dec): indistinguishable from baseline.

## What this means for how we trade

1. **Skew long in October** — this is the strongest, most robust calendar edge we have.
2. **Don't open fresh risk on a Thursday** — both majors underperform; it's a better day to
   trim or to fade a rally than to chase one.
3. **Lean risk-on into earnings-season weeks and the CNY window** — the tape has historically
   been kind there.
4. **Turn-of-month + post-quarterly-expiry (esp. ETH)** are mild long tilts.
5. **June is the soft month** — size down / less aggressive, especially ETH.
6. These are **tilts on size and timing, not standalone trades.** A +0.3–0.5%/day edge is
   real but small; it stacks with a catalyst/positioning setup, it doesn't replace one.

## Honest limits

- **9 years is thin for calendar events.** Day-of-week (~470 obs) and month (~270) are solid;
  anything holiday-specific (~8–9 obs/year) is a hint, not proof.
- **Regime-dependent.** 2017–2021 was a mega bull; Oct's strength is partly "Q4 bull-market
  timing." The vs-base t partly controls for the drift, but a calendar edge can decay.
- **Only BTC + ETH so far.** Alts have shorter history and higher beta; the next pass extends
  this to the liquid alts and adds macro dates (FOMC/CPI) and a per-coin sheet.
- Not yet backtested as an actual overlay on the strategy — this is the *measurement*; wiring
  it in and testing whether the tilt adds P&L net of fees is the follow-up.
